SellOS is billing, inventory, and GST infrastructure for Indian medical and grocery shops. This document defines how the brand looks, sounds, and shows up everywhere it appears.

01 · Foundation

What SellOS stands for

Positioning

SellOS is infrastructure, not a gadget. It doesn't try to be exciting — it tries to be trustworthy, fast, and out of the way. The shop owner is the hero; SellOS is the utility.

Personality

Calm, engineered, decisive. Reads more like a well-lit bank ATM than a colourful consumer app. Never chatty, never cute, never patronising.

Audience

Owners and billing staff of Indian medical + grocery shops. Their CAs. Ages 25–60. English + Hindi comfort. Value speed and predictability above novelty.

03 · Font exploration

Which typeface for the wordmark?

Same layered tile. Same colour system. Same letters. The typeface changes the personality more than any other single decision. Here are seven honest candidates.

sellOS
Helvetica Neue PASS

Timeless and neutral — famously so. Reads corporate / insurance / airline. On a modern POS the neutrality tips into dated. Also: not free to license commercially outside of macOS.

sellOS
Poppins ALT

Geometric, round, friendly. Enormously popular in Indian design — instantly familiar to your audience. Downside: overexposed. Every Indian SaaS/startup uses it. The 'O' being a perfect circle sits beautifully next to the S, though.

sellOS
Inter ALT

The modern SaaS default. Extremely legible at every size, huge weight range, free forever. Safe. The tradeoff: it reads generic — you'll look like every other Vercel-adjacent startup.

sellOS
Space Grotesk RECOMMENDED

Geometric with subtle character — the terminals on the lowercase letters give sell personality, while the caps stay confident for OS. Free, well-hinted, and far less used than Poppins/Inter in India. Best fit for a POS that wants to feel modern-infrastructure.

sellOS
Manrope ALT

A softer, friendlier Inter. Slight rounding gives warmth without cutesy. Solid alternative if Space Grotesk feels too editorial for your taste.

sellOS
DM Sans PASS

Utilitarian, quiet, easy to read. Great for body text, less exciting for a wordmark — the lowercase letters flatten out at logo size and the 'O' is a plain oval.

Verdict — lock Space Grotesk

Space Grotesk 700 for the wordmark. It gives OS the confident geometric shapes you want (uppercase O and S both sit clean), while the lowercase sell has enough character to not feel corporate-plain. Free on Google Fonts, well-supported everywhere, and not the shade of typography you see on every other Indian SaaS logo.

Poppins is the safe alternate if the team decides Space Grotesk feels too "design-y" and you want maximum familiarity to Indian users. Inter is the fallback if the primary font ever fails to load.

07 · Voice

Short. Specific. Never patronising. When the user has messed something up, tell them what happened and how to fix it — do not apologise, do not soften, do not personify the software.

08 · Taglines

Lines that carry the brand

One primary, three alternates. Pick one and use it consistently across landing page, App Store description, and social bios.

Primary
The system your shop runs on.

"System" ties to the OS in the name. Ownership sits with the shopkeeper ("your shop"), not the software.

Alt · Speed
Bill in seconds. File GST without stress.

Product-first. Direct benefit. Best for App Store description.

Alt · Trust
Billing, stock, and GST — one calm place.

Emphasises the "no chaos" promise. Best for landing hero if audience skews first-time software buyer.

Alt · Craft
Built for the way Indian shops actually work.

Positions against generic global POS. Best for social bios and partner decks.
