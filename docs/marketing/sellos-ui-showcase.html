Product Screens

Six screens of the running app, rebuilt to match the real components — the same #F4F4F4 canvas, #EBEBEB card borders and amber warning states the frontend actually ships.

Where these belong — and where they don't

  • Use on: the landing page, the six Facebook Page posts before the ad flight, and the field person's phone during a demo.
  • Do not use in the ad creatives. A laptop rendered at feed size is illegible on a phone, and worse, it contradicts the pitch — you sell "runs on the phone you already have, nothing to install." A MacBook tells a 50-year-old chemist he needs a computer.
  • For ads, crop a phone-sized detail instead — the expiring-soon rows for hook A, the live stock count for B, the bill in a WhatsApp bubble for C. One screen, one moment, legible at thumbnail size.
